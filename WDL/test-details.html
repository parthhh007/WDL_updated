Complete Blood Count (CBC)

Test Name: Complete Blood Count (CBC)

Purpose: To evaluate overall health and detect conditions such as anemia, infection, and blood disorders by measuring red blood cells, white blood cells, and platelets.

Sample Required: Venous blood sample (approximately 2 to 3 ml)

Preparation Instructions:
No special preparation is required.
Inform the technician if you are on any blood thinning medication.
Wear clothing that allows easy access to your arm.

Normal Reference Range:
RBC: 4.5 to 5.5 million/mcL
WBC: 4,000 to 11,000/mcL
Platelets: 150,000 to 450,000/mcL

Report Delivery Time: Same day, within 6 hours of sample collection

Important Instructions

Please carry your previous medical reports, if any, at the time of sample collection.
Inform the staff about any ongoing medications or recent illness before the test.

Fasting Requirements

Fasting is not required for this test. You may eat and drink normally before your appointment.

Note: Reference ranges may vary slightly depending on age, gender, and laboratory equipment used.
